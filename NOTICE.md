## Google News fallback

Google News RSS is used only as a fallback discovery mechanism for selected sources when their official pages cannot be read reliably. Google News is not treated as the authoritative publisher. Users should verify results against the linked original publisher page and comply with Google's applicable terms.

## Translation

The optional translation feature uses `googletrans` and/or `deep-translator`, which may send titles and summaries to external translation services. Do not process confidential, personal, or unpublished information through this feature.
